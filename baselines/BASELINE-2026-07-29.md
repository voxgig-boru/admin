## Performance baseline

Whole-process wall time, best-of-3, `--no-compile` (interpret) vs
`--force-compile` (fully compiled). SPEEDUP = interp ÷ compile (>1 = compiled
faster).

> **Caveat:** `--force-compile` bakes the one-time compilation cost into each
> wall-clock reading. For tiny suites that cost dominates the small runtime, so
> interpret wins on wall clock even though the compiled *execution* is faster.
> Property/`*_spec` suites are gated by the PBT generator machinery, which is
> roughly mode-independent (~1.0×).

Where the compiled path pays off (compute-bound work):

| suite | interp ms | compile ms | speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| sort/sort_smoke_test        | 6860 | 825 | **8.32×** |
| bloom/bloom_unit_test       | 1265 | 154 | **8.21×** |
| sort/sort_unit_test         | 6686 | 914 | **7.32×** |
| bloom/bloom_smoke_test      |  441 |  91 | **4.85×** |
| bloom/bloom_unit_spec       |  424 | 105 | **4.04×** |
| alice/aless_doc_unit_test   |  461 | 255 | 1.81× |
| sort/sort_prop_test         | 2230 |1390 | 1.60× |

Full table: `bench-out.txt` (46 rows). Interpret-favoured rows are the small
fixed-work suites where compile overhead > runtime; the large PBT specs sit at
~1.0× as expected.
